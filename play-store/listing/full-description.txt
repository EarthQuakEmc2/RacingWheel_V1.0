Racing Wheel Pro turns an Android phone into a steering wheel for PC sim racing.

Hold the phone in landscape and roll it. The wheel on screen turns with you. The left edge is the brake and the right edge is the throttle. Buttons and rotary dials sit on the wheel picture, under your thumbs, and they stay under your thumbs while the picture rotates.

A Windows receiver on the same Wi-Fi presents the phone to games as a virtual controller. ViGEm gives you an Xbox 360 pad. vJoy gives you a full 32-button device. With neither driver installed, the receiver still shows a live readout. USB works too, through adb reverse.

The price is charged by Google Play. The app does not collect a card number, and it does not run ads or analytics. The phone talks only to the PC you connect, on your own network.

Steering, pedals, buttons, the layout editor, and the PC link stay available if you never buy Premium.

This is a controller for games you already own. It is not affiliated with any car maker or wheel maker. Wheel photographs in the app are layout references. Names in the store build are generic.
